How to play

Sente moves first. Your pieces are the white tiles at the bottom; the opponent's are black tiles, turned upside down. Tap a piece, then a dotted square. To drop a captured piece, tap it in your hand (below the board), then an empty dotted square.
玉/王 K King: one step any way. 飛 R Rook: any distance straight. 角 B Bishop: any distance diagonally. 金 G Gold: one step straight or diagonally forward. 銀 S Silver: one step diagonally or straight forward. 桂 N Knight: jumps two forward and one sideways (only forward). 香 L Lance: any distance straight forward. 歩 P Pawn: one step forward.
Promotion: a move into, inside or out of the far three ranks may promote (you are asked Yes/No); it is forced when the piece could otherwise never move again (pawn/lance on the last rank, knight on the last two). Promoted pieces have a double border: 龍 +R Dragon (rook + one step diagonally), 馬 +B Horse (bishop + one step straight), と 杏 圭 全 (+P +L +N +S) move like a Gold.
Captured pieces change sides, lose their promotion and can be dropped on any empty square instead of moving, except: not where they could never move; no second unpromoted pawn in a file (nifu); a pawn drop may not give immediate checkmate (uchifuzume).
You may not leave your king in check. A player who is checkmated or has no legal move loses. The same position four times is a draw (sennichite), except that a player who gave check on every move of the repetition loses (perpetual check). No impasse (jishogi) rule. Undo takes back your last move.
