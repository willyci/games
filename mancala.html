How to play

Mancala (Kalah rules). Each player owns the six pits on their side and the store on their right: you play the bottom row and your store is on the right; the computer (or North, in two-player games) plays the top row with its store on the left. Each pit starts with 4 seeds (choose 3–6 with the seeds menu).
On your turn tap one of your pits that has seeds (framed in black). All its seeds are sown one by one counter-clockwise — to the right along your row, into your store, then along the opponent's row. The opponent's store is skipped.
Extra turn: if your last seed drops into your own store, you move again.
Capture: if your last seed lands in an empty pit on your side and the pit directly opposite holds seeds, that seed and all the opposite seeds go into your store. If the opposite pit is empty, nothing is captured.
End: as soon as all six pits on either side are empty the game ends, and each player adds the seeds left on their own side to their store. Most seeds in the store wins.
Each side numbers its pits 1–6 in the direction its seeds travel: yours from left to right, the computer's from right to left along the top. The number of the pit played last is shown in black.
