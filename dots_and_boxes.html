How to play

Take turns joining two neighbouring dots with a line (tap a dotted line, or tap inside a box near the side you want). Draw the fourth side of a box to claim it — and then you must move again. When every box is claimed, the player with the most boxes wins. Your boxes show your initial; the second player's boxes are filled black. The last line drawn is shown doubled. Tip: avoid drawing the third side of a box!
