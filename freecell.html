How to play (FreeCell Solitaire)

Objective: Move all 52 cards onto the 4 foundations (Ace to King by suit).
Rules:
  • Free Cells: The 4 top-left cells can each hold 1 card as temporary storage.
  • Foundations: The 4 top-right piles build up Ace to King in the same suit.
  • Tableau: Build down in alternating colors (e.g. Red 6 on Black 7). Any card can fill an empty column.
  • Supermove: The number of cards you can move together depends on how many free cells and empty columns are open.
  • Tap to move: Tap a card to auto-move it to a foundation, tableau, or free cell. Tap source then destination to place manually.
