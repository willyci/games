How to play (Huarong Dao / 华容道)

Objective: Move the large 2×2 Cao Cao (曹操) block to the bottom center exit.
Controls:
  • Tap to slide: Tap any block that touches an empty space. If it can only move in one direction, it slides immediately!
  • Tap to select: If a block can move in multiple directions, tap it to highlight it, then tap adjacent empty space or directional arrows.
  • Swipe: You can also swipe any block in the direction you want it to slide.
  • Blocks can never overlap, jump, or rotate. Use Undo to step back.
