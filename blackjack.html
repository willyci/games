How to play

Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Number cards count their value, J/Q/K count 10, and an Ace counts 11 or 1. Hit takes a card, Stand keeps your total, Double doubles your bet for exactly one more card, and Split turns a pair into two hands (up to 4 hands; split Aces get one card each). The dealer draws to 16 and stands on all 17s. Blackjack (Ace + 10-value card on the first two cards) pays 3 to 2, other wins pay 1 to 1, ties push.
