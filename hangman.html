How to play

Guess the hidden word one letter at a time by tapping the keys (or typing on a keyboard). Correct letters are filled in and shown in black; wrong letters are crossed out and add a part to the hangman. Six wrong guesses and the game is lost. In Two Players mode, Player 1 types a secret word and Player 2 guesses it.
