Checkers

How to play

Black (bottom) moves first; against the computer you play Black. Pieces move one square diagonally forward onto dark squares. Jump over an opponent's piece to capture it. Captures are mandatory, and if a jump lands where another jump is possible you must keep jumping with the same piece (tap each landing square in turn). A piece reaching the far row becomes a king (marked K) and may move and capture backwards too. Tap a piece to select it, then tap a dotted square to move. You win when your opponent has no pieces or no legal moves left. The game is drawn after 40 moves each with no capture and no plain piece moved.
