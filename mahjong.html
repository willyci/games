How to play (Mahjong / 麻将配对)

Solitaire Mode:
  • Tap two identical matching tiles to remove them.
  • A tile is free if it has no tiles stacked on top of it, and at least one left or right side is open. Blocked tiles are dimmed.
Connect 2 Mode (连连看):
  • Match two identical tiles that can be connected by a line with at most 2 turns through open space.
Controls:
  • Hint: Highlights an available matching pair.
  • Shuffle: Rearranges the remaining tiles (always into a clearable layout) if no moves are left.
  • Undo: Reverses your last match.
