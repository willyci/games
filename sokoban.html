How to play (Sokoban / 仓库番)

Objective: Push all crates (✕) onto the round target goals.
Rules:
  • You can only push crates; you cannot pull them.
  • You can only push one crate at a time.
  • Avoid pushing crates into dead corners where they cannot be freed!
  • Use the arrow buttons, swipe on the board, or keyboard arrow keys.
  • Use Undo to reverse any mistake.
