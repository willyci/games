How to play (Cryptogram)

Objective: Decode a famous quotation by substituting each cipher letter with its original alphabet letter.
  • Solve: Tap any cipher letter in the text to select it, then tap a letter on the keyboard (or type on your physical keyboard). All occurrences of that cipher letter will be updated automatically!
  • Delete: Tap DEL or Backspace to erase your guess for the selected letter.
  • Next letter: Tap SKIP or Tab/Arrow to move to the next unassigned letter.
  • Hint: Reveals one correct letter in the quote.
