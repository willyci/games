How to play

Players take turns placing a stone on an empty point; Black moves first. The first player to get five (or more) stones in an unbroken line — across, down or diagonally — wins, and the winning line is shaded. If the board fills up with no winner, the game is a draw. The most recent stone carries a small square mark. Watch out for your opponent's open threes and fours: block them before they become five!
