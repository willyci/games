How to play

Fill the grid so every row, every column and every 3×3 box contains the digits 1 to 9 exactly once. Tap a square to select it, then tap a number below. Bold numbers are given and cannot be changed. Turn on Notes to pencil in small candidate digits instead. Check strikes through wrong entries, Hint fills in the selected square (or a random one). The small number under each key shows how many of that digit are still to be placed. Your game is saved automatically; tap New for a fresh puzzle.
