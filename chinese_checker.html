Chinese Checkers

How to play

Black starts in the bottom point of the star and moves first; against the computer you play Black. Race all ten of your pieces into the opposite (top) point before your opponent fills yours. On your turn move one piece either one step to a neighbouring empty hole, or by hopping: jump over any single adjacent piece (yours or your opponent's) into the empty hole directly beyond it. Hops can be chained as long as you like in one turn. Tap a piece, then tap any dotted hole — the hop route is worked out for you. Pieces are never captured. You win when the target point is completely full and at least one of the pieces in it is yours (so a piece left behind cannot block you).
