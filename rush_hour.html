How to play (Rush Hour)

Objective: Drive the black ★ car out through the EXIT on the right edge of its row.
Controls:
  • Cars (2 squares, dark grey) and trucks (3 squares, light grey) only move forwards or backwards along their length — never sideways.
  • Tap a vehicle to select it (it turns white with a thick border). The squares it can reach are marked with arrows; tap one to slide it there.
  • Sliding one vehicle any distance counts as one move. The number in brackets is the fewest moves possible.
  • Hint selects the vehicle for the next move of a shortest solution and marks its destination in black.
  • Undo steps back; Restart resets the level. Solved levels get a ✓ in the list. Levels go Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced → Expert.
