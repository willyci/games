How to play

Shade squares to reveal a hidden picture. The numbers beside each row and above each column give the lengths of the runs of shaded squares in that line, in order; runs are separated by at least one blank square (0 means the line is empty). Every puzzle can be solved by logic alone, one line at a time. In Fill mode a tap shades a square; in Mark ✕ mode a tap marks a square you know is blank. Tap again to clear it. A line's numbers turn grey when its shaded squares match them. Check highlights wrong squares in grey, Hint reveals one square that can be deduced now. Pictures are hand-made 10×10 images. Your game is saved automatically.
