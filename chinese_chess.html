How to play

Red (white discs, bottom) moves first; against the computer you play Red. Pieces stand on the line crossings. Tap a piece, then tap a dotted point to move. 帥/將 General: one step along a line, never leaving the palace (the box with the X). The two generals may never face each other on an open file. 仕/士 Advisor: one step diagonally inside the palace. 相/象 Elephant: exactly two points diagonally, blocked if the point in between is occupied; cannot cross the river. 傌/馬 Horse: one step straight then one diagonally outward; blocked by a piece next to it in the direction it moves. 俥/車 Chariot: any distance along a line. 炮/砲 Cannon: moves like a chariot, but captures only by jumping over exactly one piece. 兵/卒 Soldier: one step forward; after crossing the river it may also step sideways. You may not leave your general in check. A side with no legal move (checkmate or stalemate) loses. Undo takes back your last move.
