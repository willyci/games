How to play (Dou Shou Qi / Jungle)

Board: 7 × 9 squares. Each side has a Den (穴) in the middle of its back row, guarded by three Traps (squares marked ×) beside and in front of it. The two grey Rivers (≈) fill the middle.
Pieces (rank): 象 Elephant 8, 狮 Lion 7, 虎 Tiger 6, 豹 Leopard 5, 狼 Wolf 4, 狗 Dog 3, 猫 Cat 2, 鼠 Rat 1. White discs (bottom) are yours and move first; Black discs belong to the top player.
Moving: one square up, down, left or right. Tap a piece, then a dotted square. Only the Rat may swim in the river. The Lion and Tiger may leap straight across a river (sideways or lengthways) to the first land square, capturing there if allowed — but not if any Rat is in the water on the way.
Capturing: a piece captures an enemy of equal or lower rank by moving onto it. Exception: the Rat captures the Elephant, and the Elephant cannot capture the Rat. A Rat in the water cannot capture a piece on land, and no land piece can capture it; a Rat may capture the other Rat in the water. A Rat on land may capture a Rat on land.
Traps: an enemy piece standing in one of your traps has rank 0, so any of your pieces may capture it. Your own traps do not weaken your own pieces.
A piece may not enter its own Den.
Winning: move any piece into the opponent's Den, capture all of the opponent's pieces, or leave the opponent with no legal move.
Draws: the same position with the same player to move occurring three times, or 100 moves in a row (50 each) without a capture.
The last move is marked with dashed boxes. Undo takes back your last move.
