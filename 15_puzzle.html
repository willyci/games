How to play (15 Puzzle)

Objective: Put the tiles in order — 1, 2, 3… reading left to right, top to bottom — with the empty space in the bottom-right corner.
Controls:
  • Tap a tile in the same row or column as the empty space. It slides toward the gap, pushing every tile in between along with it. One tap = one move.
  • Swipe or keyboard arrow keys also work (the tile next to the gap moves into it).
  • Undo steps back; Restart returns to the starting shuffle; New deals a fresh shuffle.
  • Hint highlights a tile to tap. On 3×3 it follows a shortest solution; on 4×4 and 5×5 it follows a quick (not shortest) plan and may give up on very scrambled boards.
  • “Placed tiles: grey” shades tiles already in their final spot.
  • Every shuffle is guaranteed solvable. Your best (fewest moves) is kept per size.
