How to play

Uncover every square that does not hide a mine. A number tells how many mines touch that square (including diagonals). Choose Dig to uncover squares or Flag to mark squares you think hide a mine (tap a flag again in Flag mode to remove it). Your first tap is always safe. Tapping an uncovered number whose neighbouring flags match it opens all its other neighbours. Beginner has 10 mines, Intermediate 24, Expert 40. Your game is saved automatically; tap New to start over.
