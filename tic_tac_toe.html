How to play

X always moves first, then the players take turns marking an empty square. The first to get three marks in a row — across, down or diagonally — wins, and the winning line is shown on black squares. If all nine squares fill up with no line, it is a draw. The most recent mark is underlined. On Hard the computer plays perfectly: the best you can do is a draw! The score tally is kept on this device; tap Reset score to clear it.
