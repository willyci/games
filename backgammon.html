How to play

You play the white checkers and move them counter-clockwise: along the top from right to left, then along the bottom from left to right, into your home board at the bottom right (points 1–6). Tap Roll, then tap one of your points (or the bar) and tap a highlighted destination (dashed outline, inverted number). Each die is one move; doubles give four moves. You must use both dice if you can; if only one can be used you must use the larger one if possible. A single checker (a blot) that is landed on is hit and goes to the bar; checkers on the bar must re-enter in the opponent's home board before anything else moves. A point held by two or more opposing checkers is blocked. Once all 15 of your checkers are in your home board you may bear them off by tapping the Off box; a die higher than your farthest checker may bear off from the highest occupied point. Tap Done to end your turn when no moves remain, or Undo to take back moves made this turn. First to bear off all 15 wins (a gammon if the loser has borne off none). The numbers show point numbers from the moving player's side.
