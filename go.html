How to play

Black and White take turns placing a stone on an empty intersection; Black moves first. A stone or group of connected stones (joined along the lines) is captured and removed when it has no empty neighbouring points left. You may not play a move that leaves your own group with no liberties (unless it captures), and you may not immediately retake a single stone in a ko — play elsewhere first.

When you think the game is over, press Pass. After two passes in a row the game stops: tap any group that is dead (cannot avoid capture) to mark it — it turns grey with a dashed edge; tap again to unmark. Then press Score. Area scoring is used: each side gets one point for every stone on the board plus every empty point surrounded only by its own stones. White gets 6.5 points komi. Small squares show each side's territory. Undo during marking returns to play.
