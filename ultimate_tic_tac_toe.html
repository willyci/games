How to play

The board is a 3×3 grid of small tic-tac-toe boards. X moves first, anywhere. After that, the square you pick inside a small board sends your opponent to the matching small board: play the top-right square and your opponent must play in the top-right board. The board(s) where the next mark may go have a thick black frame and white squares; all other squares are grey. If you are sent to a board that is already won or full, you may play in any open board. Get three in a row in a small board to win it: it is covered by a big X or O. A full small board with no line is drawn (marked –) and counts for nobody. Win three small boards in a row — across, down or diagonally — to win the game (the winning boards turn black). If no moves are left and nobody has three in a row, the player who won more small boards wins; equal counts are a draw. The most recent mark is underlined.
