How to play (Klondike Solitaire)

Objective: Build all 4 foundations from Ace to King by suit.
Rules:
  • Tableau: Build down in alternating colors (e.g. Black 8 on Red 9). Only Kings can fill empty columns.
  • Tap to move: Tap a card to automatically move it to a foundation or valid column. You can also tap a card then tap the destination.
  • Stock: Tap the top-left deck to draw cards. When empty, tap to recycle.
  • Auto button: Clears all remaining cards once all face-down cards are uncovered!
