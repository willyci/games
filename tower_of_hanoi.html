How to play

Move the whole tower from the left peg to another peg. Tap a peg to pick up its top disc (it is lifted above the peg and turns black), then tap the peg you want to drop it on. Tap the same peg again to put it back. Only one disc moves at a time, and a larger disc may never be placed on a smaller one. The fewest possible moves for n discs is 2n − 1. Stuck? Tap Solve, then Next step (or tap the board) to watch the best solution one move at a time, continuing from wherever you are (it heads for whichever free peg is quicker, normally the right one). Undo leaves solve mode.
