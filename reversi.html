How to play

Black moves first. Place a disc so that it traps one or more of your opponent's discs in a straight line (across, down or diagonally) between the new disc and another of your discs. All trapped discs flip to your colour. Small dots show the legal moves. If you have no legal move you must pass; the game ends when neither player can move. Whoever has more discs wins. The most recently placed disc carries a small square mark. Corners can never be flipped — they are valuable!
