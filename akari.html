How to play

Place light bulbs (●) in the white squares so that every white square is lit. A bulb lights its whole row and column until the light hits a black wall. Two bulbs may never shine on each other. A wall with a number must have exactly that many bulbs directly next to it (above, below, left, right); walls without numbers can have any amount. Tap a square to cycle: empty → bulb → ✕ (no bulb here) → empty. Lit squares are grey. Bulbs that shine on each other are shown inverted, and a wall with too many (or no room for enough) bulbs is shown grey and underlined. Check marks wrong bulbs and marks, Hint places one correct bulb. Every puzzle has exactly one solution. Your game is saved automatically.
