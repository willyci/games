How to play

Players take turns dropping a disc into one of the seven columns; it falls to the lowest empty space. Tap anywhere in a column (or the ▼ button above it) to drop your disc. Black (●) always moves first, White (○) second. The first player to line up four of their discs in a row — across, up and down, or diagonally — wins, and the winning four are shown on gray squares. If the board fills up with no four in a row, the game is a draw. The most recent disc carries a small square mark.
