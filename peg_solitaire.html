How to play

Tap a peg to select it (it turns white on black). Holes it can jump into show a dot — tap one to jump. A peg jumps over a neighbouring peg into an empty hole straight beyond it, and the jumped peg is removed. On the square boards jumps go up, down, left or right; on the triangle they also go along the diagonals. Leave as few pegs as possible — ideally just one (on the English board, in the centre).
