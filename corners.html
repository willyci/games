How to play

Each player has 12 pieces in a corner (shaded). White starts bottom-left, Black top-right; against the computer you play White and move first. Race to move all your pieces into your opponent's starting corner. On your turn move one piece either one step up, down, left or right to an empty square, or jump over a neighbouring piece (any colour) into the empty square right behind it. Jumps can be chained in any direction; all reachable squares are dotted, so just tap the final destination. Nothing is captured. If White finishes first, Black gets one last move: if Black also finishes, it is a draw.
